# Gesture FX / Visual Shows

Status: family index. Second recommended implementation after Gesture Retail Commerce.

Goal: use webcam gestures, camera presence, audio and visual effects to create branded attention loops for storefronts, MUPIs, events and campaign screens.

## Why this family matters

Gesture FX is fast to understand and easy to sell:

- people move their hands and the screen reacts;
- brand color, particles, logo, CTA and QR can be swapped quickly;
- it works for short dwell-time environments such as windows, MUPIs, fairs and festivals.

## Source repos and modules

- `gesture-lab/camera-fx-cum-laude/` - current webcam FX family.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/hand-gesture-particle-effects` - particle gestures.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/webcam-audio-visualizer` - webcam/audio/Three.js visualizer reference.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/SwordArt` - gesture-controlled 3D particle effect reference.
- `gesture-lab/mirrora/display-mupi-live/` - strong personalization pattern for display/MUPI.

## Suggested first product

Gesture FX Visual Show v1:

- attract loop;
- consent/camera start;
- live camera or silhouette;
- hand-triggered visual effects;
- brand palette/logo/claim;
- QR and CTA;
- 16:9 / 9:16 modes;
- capture/export PNG or short clip when supported.

## What not to do yet

- Do not publish every effect as a separate module.
- Do not overcomplicate with heavy 3D before proving the basic visual loop.
- Do not hide CTA/QR behind the visual spectacle.

## Next decision

Use Camera FX and MIRRORA MUPI patterns to package one branded visual-show module, then add effect presets inside it.
