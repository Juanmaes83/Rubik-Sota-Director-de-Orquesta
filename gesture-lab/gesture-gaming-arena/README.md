# Gesture Gaming Arena

Status: family index. Third recommended implementation after Retail Commerce and Gesture FX.

Goal: package webcam-controlled games as short commercial challenges for events, stands, stores, schools and brand activations.

## Why this family matters

Gaming creates participation and memory:

- users understand the challenge quickly;
- brands can sponsor levels, rewards, rankings and QR follow-up;
- it can turn webcam interaction into a measurable event loop.

## Source repos and modules

- `gesture-lab/gesture-gaming/` - current Rubik launcher.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/Hand-Gesture-Gaming` - 3D character/world gesture reference.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/Real-Time-Hand-Gesture-Control-System-for-Racing-Games` - racing control reference.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/Hand-Gesture-Controlled-Game` - simple game gesture reference.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/hand-gesture-game-controller` - keyboard/control bridge reference.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/Starter-Kit-Racing2` - possible racing receiver/game base.
- `https://github.com/Juanmaes83/gesture-cube` - Rubik/cube challenge reference.

## Suggested first products

- Gesture Racing Challenge: 30-60 second branded racing game with ranking and QR.
- Rubik Gesture Cube Challenge: cube/gesture challenge for education, events and brand recall.
- Gesture Adventure Arena: character movement challenge, later phase.

## Target sectors

- automotive;
- gaming;
- shopping centers;
- education;
- tourism family events;
- fairs;
- sports retail;
- brand activations.

## QA concerns

- latency;
- camera permission;
- lighting;
- gesture ambiguity;
- fairness and scoring;
- mobile vs desktop performance;
- fallback controls.

## Next decision

Do not develop first. Keep as a documented family until Retail Commerce and Gesture FX are stable.
